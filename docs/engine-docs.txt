@section engine_cli CLI options

On startup, the engine will process any command-line arguments as if they were a single UCI command.

The following additional flags may also be specified:
@li ``--no-loop``: processes the initial UCI command line and then exits, without entering the UCI event loop. Useful for scripts.

These flags may be given before or after the other CLI arguments.
